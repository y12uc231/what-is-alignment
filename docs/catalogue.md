# Catalogue of cases

This table lists all 265 documented cases in the repository with their verdicts under the rules of [Section 1](01-first-principles.md). It is generated by `scripts/build_catalogue.py` from the case cards in `data/cases/` (each with verbatim, checked quotes from its sources) and the verdicts in `data/verdicts/`; `data/cases_final.yaml` has the same content in machine-readable form. Verdicts are stated relative to a principal. Cases that several sources describe appear once, with all sources listed.

Counts: 71 alignment failures, 31 misaligned objectives (optimizers with no goals of their own), 92 boundary cases, 9 model organisms (built by researchers to study misalignment), 62 not alignment failures.
